1. IDENTIFIED KNOWLEDGE GAPS & SYSTEM MITIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- L2/L3 Curriculum Detail:
  - Gap: Only topic weights and pathway names (Portfolio Management, Private Wealth,
    Private Markets) are available. Deep reading and LOS lists do not exist.
  - Mitigation: The Vector Store Manager will seed a structured placeholder file in
    the curriculum index indicating Level II/III topic weights. If a candidate asks
    a deep LOS question on Level II/III, the Tutor Agent will retrieve this chunk
    and respond: "Detailed Level II/III curriculum contents are not fully indexed
    in this workspace. Please refer to cfainstitute.org."
- Approved Calculator Models:
  - Gap: No official approved calculator model list exists in the source files.
  - Mitigation: The FAQ database and policies index will be seeded with standard
    synthetic listings: "Texas Instruments BA II Plus" and "Hewlett Packard 12C".
- Approved ID Document List:
  - Gap: Explicit matrix of accepted country IDs is missing.
  - Mitigation: The policies index will state: "A valid, international passport is
    the only accepted ID for registration and exam entry."
- Disability Accommodation List:
  - Gap: No detailed disability accommodation listing.
  - Mitigation: Seeding options like: "Extra Exam Time (1.5x / 2.0x)", "Visual Assistive
    Technology", and "Reader/Scribe".

2. SYNTHETIC DATA GENERATION PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A seed script `scripts/seed_db.py` will generate the following synthetic records
in SQLite:
- candidates: 3 candidate profiles (John Smith - Level I, Sarah Chen - Level II,
  David Lee - Level III Private Wealth) and 1 Admin.
- enrollments: Pre-populated paid and unpaid enrollments.
- exam_slots: Held/confirmed slots at centers or remote.
- study_plans: Seeding default topic sequence for Level I based on weight-ordering.
- content_progress: Seeding mock reading progress records.
- mock_exams: Score histories ranging from 55% to 78% with seeded weak area lists
  (e.g., ["Quantitative Methods", "Fixed Income"]).
- questions: Seeding >=5 mock MCQs per Level I topic.
- support_tickets: Sample ticket database with "open", "in_progress", and "resolved".

No real PII (names, phone numbers, passport numbers, card details) will be used.
All records will use standard test email domains (e.g. @example.com).
