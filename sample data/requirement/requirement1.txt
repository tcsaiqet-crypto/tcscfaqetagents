================================================================================
Business Requirements & Acceptance Criteria
================================================================================
Document Title: CFA Digital Candidate Journey BRD
Classification: TCS Confidential
Scope: End-to-End Digital Candidate Journey for CFA Examination & Learning
       Ecosystem (Covering the full candidate lifecycle — discovery,
       enrollment, learning, examination, certification, and continuous
       engagement)

================================================================================
Business Requirements Matrix
================================================================================

Req ID  Business Requirement
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BR-01   Candidate Registration & Digital Onboarding
        A prospective candidate must be able to register and onboard onto
        the CFA platform through a single, guided digital journey.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Candidate can self-register via web/mobile with email or social/SSO login.
        • Guided onboarding captures profile, education, and program interest.
        • Account is created and a personalized dashboard is provisioned within
          3 seconds of successful sign-up.

BR-02   Identity Verification & KYC
        Candidate identity must be verified digitally before enrollment and
        examination to ensure authenticity and compliance.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Government-ID and biometric/liveness checks are supported.
        • Verification status is recorded and auditable.
        • Failed or suspicious verifications are flagged and routed for manual review.

BR-03   Program & Exam Discovery
        Candidate must be able to explore CFA levels, curriculum, eligibility,
        and exam windows relevant to their profile.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • System displays Level I/II/III details, prerequisites, and fees.
        • Eligibility is validated against candidate profile before enrollment.
        • Available exam windows and locations are shown in real time.

BR-04   Enrollment & Fee Payment
        Candidate must be able to enroll for an exam level and complete secure
        fee payment online.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Multiple payment methods (card, net-banking, wallet) are supported.
        • Payment is processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateway with instant receipt.
        • Enrollment is confirmed and reflected in the candidate dashboard on success.

BR-05   AI-Driven Personalized Learning Path
        The learning ecosystem must generate a tailored study plan based on
        candidate profile, level, and available study time.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • System recommends a level-appropriate study path mapped to the curriculum.
        • Plan adapts to target exam date and weekly study capacity.
        • Each recommendation maps to specific topics/learning outcomes.

BR-06   Digital Learning Content & LMS
        Candidate must be able to access structured curriculum content, videos,
        and reading materials within an integrated LMS.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Curriculum is organized by topic, reading, and learning outcome statement.
        • Content supports web and mobile with offline access where applicable.
        • Completion and time-on-content are tracked per candidate.

BR-07   AI Study Assistant / Tutor
        Candidate must be able to ask questions and receive contextual
        explanations from an AI tutor during preparation.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Assistant answers curriculum-based questions with grounded, accurate responses.
        • Explanations reference the relevant topic/reading.
        • Candidate can request practice questions or clarifications on demand.

BR-08   Practice Tests & Mock Examinations
        Candidate must be able to take timed practice tests and full-length mock
        exams that mirror the real exam.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Mock exams replicate exam format, timing, and difficulty.
        • Automated scoring with topic-level performance breakdown is provided.
        • Weak areas are identified and fed back into the learning path.

BR-09   Exam Slot Scheduling & Booking
        Candidate must be able to schedule, reschedule, or cancel an exam
        appointment digitally.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Real-time availability of dates, centers, and remote slots is shown.
        • Booking, rescheduling, and cancellation follow policy rules and windows.
        • Confirmation and calendar reminders are issued automatically.

BR-10   Secure Exam Delivery & Proctoring
        The examination must be delivered securely with center-based or remote
        AI-assisted proctoring.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Exam launches only after identity re-verification.
        • AI proctoring detects and logs integrity violations in real time.
        • Exam content and responses are encrypted end to end.

BR-11   Results Processing & Score Reporting
        Candidate must receive accurate, timely exam results with a clear
        performance summary.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Results are published within the committed SLA window.
        • Score report shows pass/fail and topic-level performance bands.
        • Results are accessible and downloadable from the dashboard.

BR-12   Digital Certification & Credentialing
        Successful candidates must receive a verifiable digital certificate/badge
        upon meeting requirements.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Tamper-proof, verifiable digital credential is issued automatically.
        • Credential is shareable to professional networks (e.g., LinkedIn).
        • Third parties can validate authenticity via a verification link.

BR-13   Progress Tracking & Analytics Dashboard
        Candidate must be able to monitor learning progress, readiness, and exam
        outcomes in one place.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Dashboard shows study progress, mock scores, and readiness indicators.
        • Analytics highlight strengths, gaps, and recommended next actions.
        • Data updates in near real-time as the candidate progresses.

BR-14   Notifications & Omni-Channel Communication
        Candidate must receive timely reminders and updates across the journey
        via multiple channels.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Automated reminders for deadlines, exams, and study milestones are sent.
        • Notifications are delivered via email, SMS, push, and in-app.
        • Candidate can manage channel and frequency preferences.

BR-15   Support, Helpdesk & Chatbot Assistance
        Candidate must be able to obtain help across registration, learning, and
        exam processes.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • AI chatbot resolves common queries and escalates complex ones.
        • Human support is reachable with full context handover.
        • Support interactions are logged and tracked to resolution.

BR-16   Accessibility & Inclusion
        The platform must be accessible to candidates with diverse needs and
        provide accommodations.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • Interface meets WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
        • Exam accommodations (extra time, assistive tech) can be requested and applied.
        • Content supports multiple languages/locales where applicable.

BR-17   Data Privacy, Security & Compliance
        Candidate data across the ecosystem must be protected and processed in a
        compliant manner.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • PII is encrypted in transit and at rest with role-based, audited access.
        • Processing complies with GDPR and applicable data-protection standards.
        • Consent and data-retention policies are enforced and traceable.

BR-18   Platform Reliability & Scalability
        The ecosystem must remain reliable and performant during peak enrollment
        and exam periods.
        Acceptance Criteria:
        • System scales to peak load without degradation of experience.
        • Uptime meets agreed SLA (e.g., ≥99.9%) with self-healing on transient faults.
        • Failover and recovery events are logged without candidate-facing disruption.

================================================================================
Business Outcomes Addressed
================================================================================
• Seamless digital candidate experience
• Higher enrollment & completion rates
• Improved exam integrity & trust
• Personalized, outcome-driven learning
• Faster results & credentialing
• Scalable, reliable, compliant operations
